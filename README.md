# Hyundai Sales Performance Analysis | Tableau

Find the live interactive version here: https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/nada.nabeeh/viz/HYUNDAICARSANALYSIS/Dashboard1#1

This Tableau project analyzes Hyundai car sales across different models and markets. It focuses on sales performance, customer preferences, and market trends to support better business decisions.

## Key Insights

**Top-Selling Model:** Elantra is the best-selling model with 456 units sold.

**Top Market:** Saudi Arabia has the highest sales with 352 units.

**Popular Car Color:** White is the most popular car color with 764 units sold.

**Customer Income:** The average annual customer income is $279,860.

## Business Recommendations

**Focus on Top Models:** Increase marketing for popular sedan models such as Elantra and Accent.

**Expand in Saudi Arabia:** Strengthen dealership presence in the top-performing market.

**Increase Female Customer Engagement:** Create marketing campaigns to attract more female customers.

**Focus on Popular Colors:** Give more attention to white and grey cars based on customer preferences.

**Target Higher-Income Customers:** Offer premium features and flexible financing plans to attract mid-to-high income customers.
